# Versus combat design — approved direction

Host: Risk of Rain Returns (requires local compatibility verification). Inspiration: Brawlhalla (not a required game).

- First fighters: Commando (dual pistols, ranged) and Mercenary (sword, melee).
- Signature weapons only; no pickups.
- Brawlhalla-style light, heavy, aerial, and dodge actions.
- Hybrid eliminations: zero health OR ring-out. Damage increases knockback.
- One life per round, first to three round wins (best of five).
- Classic arena: one main platform, two elevated platforms, open sides, lower blast zone.

The JSON sheets in `design/` are the source of truth. All mechanics are design-only until loader compatibility, game APIs, values, and tests are verified. Attack timings, damage, arena coordinates, and networking implementation remain unverified and deliberately unspecified.
